# RENA Verification Scope

## RENA can establish
- Behavioral/statistical forgetting evidence under the recorded protocol.
- Reference-relative similarity and verifier agreement.
- Tested adversarial robustness where those checks were executed.
- Audit provenance and reproducibility metadata.
- Cryptographic integrity/authenticity of RENA-issued certificates.

## RENA cannot establish
- Physical deletion of model parameters.
- Universal unlearning for unseen architectures or datasets.
- Perfect information deletion.
- Generalization beyond the evaluated configuration.

A PASS is evidence consistent with effective unlearning under the tested conditions, not a universal proof of erasure.
